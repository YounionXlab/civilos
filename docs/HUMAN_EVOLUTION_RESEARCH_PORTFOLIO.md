# X-LAB Human Evolution Research Portfolio

## Purpose

This document adds portfolio-management fields to the canonical `HUMAN_EVOLUTION_RESEARCH_AGENDA.md`.

The Research Agenda preserves the important questions. The Research Portfolio determines which questions X-LAB is actively advancing, how mature each track is, and which experiments currently support it.

The portfolio adds three canonical fields to every `Q-xxx` question:

- **Priority** — how important the question is to X-LAB now;
- **Maturity** — how far the research track has progressed;
- **Active Experiments** — current models, simulations, prototypes, or live experiments connected to the question.

---

## Priority Scale

### P0 — Active Strategic Priority

Directly connected to current X-LAB products, active experiments, external opportunities, or foundational research infrastructure.

### P1 — High Priority

Important to the X-LAB mission and likely to receive active research or experimental work in the near term.

### P2 — Watch / Build Foundations

Important long-term question. Preserve evidence, models, and opportunities, but do not allocate major implementation effort yet.

### P3 — Horizon

Long-range research question kept in the agenda to preserve strategic option value.

Priority is not a judgment of absolute importance to humanity. It is an X-LAB resource-allocation field.

---

## Maturity Scale

### M0 — Question

Question is registered but not yet structurally modeled.

### M1 — Framed

Initial variables, mechanisms, uncertainties, and research directions are identified.

### M2 — Modeled

A structured model, world model, simulation model, or formal experimental design exists.

### M3 — Experimental

At least one reproducible experiment, prototype, simulation, or observational workflow is running.

### M4 — Validated

Multiple runs, external evidence, or real-world feedback have produced stable findings.

### M5 — Operational

The research has been converted into a repeatable decision, product, policy, engineering, or deployment capability.

Maturity is domain-specific. A question can be scientifically mature while its X-LAB implementation remains early.

## Portfolio Operating Rules

### 1. Questions are stable; priorities are dynamic

A `Q-xxx` identifier should remain stable even when its priority changes.

### 2. Do not confuse Priority with Maturity

A P0 question may still be M0 if it has just become strategically urgent.

A P2 question may already have mature external science but little X-LAB implementation.

### 3. Every P0 question should have a next concrete artifact

Examples:

- research memo;
- dataset;
- model;
- experiment definition;
- prototype;
- simulation;
- real-world pilot;
- decision framework.

### 4. Every active experiment should reference one or more `Q-xxx` IDs

Example:

```text
EXP-CIV-001 Ares Alpha

Primary:
Q-024 Sustainable Mars Civilization

Secondary:
Q-023 First Cross-body Civilization System
Q-027 Civilization Resilience and Great Filter
Q-028 Civilization Quality
Q-029 Multi-planet Civilization
```

### 5. New projects should not automatically create new questions

First attempt to map the project to the existing Research Agenda. Create a new `Q-xxx` only when the underlying research question is genuinely distinct.

### 6. Portfolio review should be periodic

Recommended cadence:

- lightweight review monthly;
- full priority and maturity review quarterly;
- immediate review after a major external event, experiment result, or strategic opportunity.

## Next Portfolio Milestone

The next structural milestone is to make the portfolio machine-readable.

Recommended canonical data structure:

```text
research/
  questions.json
  mappings.json
```

Each question should eventually contain:

```json
{
  "id": "Q-024",
  "title": "Sustainable Mars Civilization",
  "domains": ["civilization", "space"],
  "priority": "P0",
  "maturity": "M3",
  "active_experiments": ["EXP-CIV-001"],
  "status": "active"
}
```

That will allow the X-LAB website, CivilOS, YICE, project management, and future research agents to consume the same canonical Research Agenda.

## Thesis

The Research Agenda answers:

> What should humanity understand?

The Research Portfolio answers:

> Which of those questions should X-LAB move forward now, and how?

Together they turn the Human Evolution Key Questions from a static list into a living research system.
